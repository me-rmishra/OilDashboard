    How to read this. Prices are delayed front-month futures from Yahoo Finance. Cracks are computed: gasoline crack = RBOB×42 − Brent; diesel crack = ULSD×42 − Brent; 3-2-1 = (2×RBOB + 1×ULSD)×42 − 3×crude, ÷3. The GRM proxy assumes a simple yield (50% gasoline, 40% diesel, 10% residue at 60% of crude) and is a US-Gulf-style indicator, not Singapore GRM or Reliance GRM. Physical differentials (Dubai, Murban, Urals, Basrah) and Singapore cracks need Platts/Argus. Brent–WTI here is a futures spread, a proxy only.
